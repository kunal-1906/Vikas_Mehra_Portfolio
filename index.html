03 — Profile

A print-trained eye
for digital commerce.

Creative and detail-oriented graphic designer with five years across packaging, branding, and digital content. I blend artistic vision with production precision — colour-separated files that hold on press, and A+ modules that hold on Amazon.

Currently Assistant Art Manager at Netplus Broadband, coordinating campaign creatives for broadband, TV, and OTT. Previously led packaging and A+ at Vinsun Enterprises, rehabilitation product packaging at Medi Surgi Solutions, and print-ready identity work at Malik Printers.

Approach

Strong conceptual thinking with a focus on brand identity. Balance creativity with function. Adapt tone for hosiery racks, hospital packs, and premium e-comm alike.

How I work

Collaborative, deadline-true, and meticulous. Ideas get refined through feedback. Fast pace without losing the file that goes to the printer.
